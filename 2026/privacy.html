Privacy

Advent Village is an audiobook advent calendar for the Cover to Cover YouTube channel (@C2CAudiobooks). This page says what personal data the site uses.

Who is responsible

Pranav Raut, Gubener Str. 13D, 10243 Berlin, Germany. Email: pranavraut033@gmail.com.

What we collect

There are no forms or accounts. We don't ask you for any information.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). To deliver the pages and keep them secure, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details and may keep them in its server logs. The transfer to the USA rests on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, under which GitHub is certified. Basis: our legitimate interest in delivering the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). All code, models and the 3D library are served from this site; nothing else loads unless you accept analytics.

Google Analytics, only if you accept

If you click Accept on the cookie banner, the page loads Google Analytics 4 from Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC to count visits. Google sets cookies (_ga, _ga_<id>) that last up to 400 days (about 13 months) after your last visit. It receives which pages you view, your device and browser, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. Data can be transferred to Google LLC in the USA, which is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Google Signals and ad personalisation are switched off, and there are no adverts.

Basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG). Google Analytics keeps event and user data for 2 months; after that only aggregated reports remain. If you click Reject, nothing from Google loads.

Changing your mind

Click "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the village page at any time. Choosing Reject after accepting deletes the Analytics cookies and stops Google from loading.

Stored on your device

  • Your cookie choice, in your browser's local storage (advent-village-consent), so we don't ask again.
  • Whether you have seen the channel note, until you close the tab (advent-village-credit).

Neither holds an identifier, and neither is sent anywhere.

YouTube

The buttons that go to YouTube are ordinary links. Nothing from YouTube loads on this site until you click one. After that, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction, erasure or restriction of your data, and object to its use, by writing to the email above. You can withdraw consent at any time. You can complain to a data protection authority, for example the one where you live. The authority for us is the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.
