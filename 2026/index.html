    Credits

    Advent Village 2026 is an audiobook advent calendar for the Cover to Cover YouTube channel: one classic book a day from 1 to 24 December 2026.

    Made by
    Pranav Raut, with Claude (Anthropic) writing the code and building the 3D pipeline.
    Readings
    Public-domain books read for Cover to Cover. The voices are generated with OmniVoice and Qwen voice clones, designed for each book.
    Houses and tree
    Pictures made in Google Flow (Nano Banana), turned into 3D models with Hyper3D Rodin (Gen-2.5 High).
    Props
    Lamps, fences, presents, snowmen, pines and the island rock, made in Meshy.
    Blender
    Used to measure and compress the models and to repaint the sign lettering.
    Village layout
    Placed by a small Python script; the paths, gardens and extra trees are laid out in the page itself.
    Site
    Built with three.js and hosted on GitHub Pages.
